01 — What we do

One lab. One inspection team. Zero guesswork.

Testing tells you what a product is made of and how it behaves. Inspection tells you whether the production run matches. Labtech does both, independently and impartially.

Physical testing

How a material holds up when it is pulled, rubbed, washed and worn.

  • Material analysis & fibre composition
  • Tensile, tear & seam strength
  • Abrasion, pilling & functional tests
  • Dimensional stability & appearance after washing
  • Colour fastness — wash, rub, perspiration, light
  • Zipper, bag, suitcase & umbrella testing
Run a simulated test →

Chemical testing

What is in the product — and whether it is safe against the limits of your market.

  • Azo dyes in textiles & leather
  • Phthalates in prints & coatings
  • Chlorophenols & biocidal preservatives
  • Organotin compounds in footwear
  • pH value & formaldehyde
  • Saliva & perspiration fastness
See where substances hide →

Toy testing

Toys get the strictest look: small parts, flames and anything a child might put in their mouth.

  • Mechanical & physical properties
  • Flammability
  • Migration of certain elements
  • Organic chemical compounds

    07 — Accreditation

    Independently verified. Twice.

    A test report is only as good as the lab behind it. Labtech's competence is assessed by national accreditation bodies against the two international standards that matter for this work.

    ISO/IEC 17025:2017

    Testing laboratory · accredited by the Bangladesh Accreditation Board (BAB)

    The international standard for the competence, impartiality and consistent operation of testing laboratories — covering staff, equipment, validated methods and the traceability of every result.

    ISO/IEC 17020:2012 — Type A

    Inspection body · accredited by NABCB, India

    Type A is the highest independence category: an inspection body with no involvement in the design, manufacture, supply or purchase of what it inspects.

    Independent

    No stake in the outcome of any test or inspection.

    Impartial

    The same method and the same judgement for every client.

    Traceable

    Every result linked to a specimen, a method and an analyst.
